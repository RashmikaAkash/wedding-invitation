How we got here

Our Story

❦

2019 · First Meeting

A rainy day in Kandy

We met at a little bookshop café while sheltering from the rain. One shared umbrella and a very long conversation about books later, neither of us wanted the rain to stop.

2022 · First Trip

Chasing sunsets in Ella

Our first trip together — misty train rides, Nine Arches Bridge at dawn, and the moment we both quietly knew this was something lasting.

2025 · The Proposal

She said yes

Under string lights in the same garden where we'll marry, Akash got down on one knee. Through happy tears, Amiduni said the easiest "yes" of her life.

2026 · Forever Begins

And now, with you

The best chapter is the one we write surrounded by the people we love most. Thank you for being part of our story.
